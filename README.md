Keeping my notes open-source helps me study anywhere and allows my peers to access these resources easily!

- **Database Management Systems (DBMS)**
  - Solved Question Banks (Units 1 & 2)

- **Analysis and Design of Algorithms (ADA)**
  - Objective and Subjective Solved Banks (Units 1 & 2)
